About

Connecting technical depth with investment judgment.

My work sits at the intersection of technology, investment, and finance. I have conducted research across semiconductor equipment, advanced packaging, optoelectronic chips, AI data-center infrastructure, energy storage, and advanced manufacturing.

Across internships at Hillhouse, Tencent Investment, CICC Capital, and CSC Securities, I have developed experience in sector mapping, company analysis, financial modeling, investment memo drafting, management interviews, and deal execution.

I am especially interested in helping technology-driven companies move from primary-market innovation to public-market financing and sustainable commercial scale.
